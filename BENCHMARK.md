# Meeting and broadcast mobile scorecard

Scored from the mobile pass in `QUIRKS.md`, not from a feature checklist. Scale is 1–5. A 5 means that audience would rather use this than the product that usually wins for them. We do not lead any audience yet.

Competitors this is held against: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard, YouTube Live, and church streaming desks (Resi, BoxCast, ProPresenter-style “go live” panels).

## Where they still beat us

- **Zoom / Meet / Teams.** The hang-up, mute, and camera controls stay inside the screen on a 320px phone. Chat is a sheet or a column that does not sit under the call dock. Reconnecting is a banner, not a truncated sentence.
- **FaceTime.** One primary action, huge targets, no password essay, the preview is the screen.
- **StreamYard / YouTube Live.** A broadcast is either LIVE or it is not. The host sees that before they look at a status sentence. Viewers joining late see “live” or “offline”, not “Joining…”.
- **Church desks.** A volunteer names the service and goes on. This path still asks for one host password before the room exists.

Hang up stays on screen (Q1). Chat sits above the dock, including under a keyboard (Q2). A broadcast chip says RECONNECTING, OFF AIR, LIVE, or WATCHING (Q5). Create & enter stays in the card while the room fields scroll (Q4). More scrolls inside a short landscape screen (Q3). The chip and the dock clear a notch and a home indicator (Q7). The status line and the chip title grow with the root text size (Q6, Q13). Flip sits on the phone dock (Q5b). Create asks for one host password (Q12). The dock gap is at least 8px (Q10). Continue is the only filled join action (Q11).

Corporate and personal are 3.9. Church is 3.7. None lead. Large type is 4 for every audience: a 32px root paints the phone title at 40px and the status at 34px, and Fit sits under the chip. It is not a 5. Fit’s label is still 14px, and FaceTime still wins that dimension. Landscape stays at 4: Meet is still the meeting a corporate user would rotate into. Warm copy for church stays at 3: a host password is still required before the room exists. Live, off air, watching, and reconnecting stay at 3.

## Next gap

Live, off air, watching, and reconnecting are a small badge inside the chip. A late viewer and a reconnecting host still read a status sentence to learn the state. YouTube Live and StreamYard put that state in a banner.
