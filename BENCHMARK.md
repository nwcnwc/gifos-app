# Meeting and broadcast mobile scorecard

Scored from the mobile pass in `QUIRKS.md`, not from a feature checklist. Scale is 1–5. A 5 means that audience would rather use this than the product that usually wins for them. We do not lead any audience yet.

Competitors this is held against: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard, YouTube Live, and church streaming desks (Resi, BoxCast, ProPresenter-style “go live” panels).

## Where they still beat us

- **Zoom / Meet / Teams.** The hang-up, mute, and camera controls stay inside the screen on a 320px phone. Chat is a sheet or a column that does not sit under the call dock.
- **FaceTime.** One primary action, huge targets, the preview is the screen. On a tall phone the picture is that screen, and a tap on it is Go live (Q59). Watch and the host key open from More. FaceTime still wins the call: one control, and almost nothing else on the glass.
- **StreamYard / YouTube Live.** The state is a banner now (Q14). The picture leads a phone before Go live (Q44). A quiet viewer reads that the service or stream has not started, and an emptied room reads that it has ended (Q54). A recording on the phone plays back (Q60). A speaker fills the phone, and the frame stays whole (Q61). A viewer who watched can watch it again (Q62), and the host or that viewer can Share the video to whoever missed it (Q68). Go live keeps every service without a Record tap, and Leave hands the host that replay (Q69). The host sets a start time in More. The link says “starts Sunday at 10:00 AM” with Add to calendar, and an old link reads that it has ended (Q70). Someone who opens the link after it ended reads that, and Watch plays it from anyone still in the room who holds it; then that phone holds it too (Q71). If nobody watched, the host's Leave keeps the seat while the replay is open, and the late arrival plays the host's kept copy (Q72). Once that sheet is closed and every holder has left, the link still cannot play it. A viewer reads Give on the picture, and Tip on a stream. It opens the host's own giving page, and it is still there on the screen that says the service ended (Q73).
- **Church desks.** A viewer’s join says they are seen only if they raise a hand and are brought on stage (Q52). Give sits on the picture and opens the church’s own giving page, as on Resi and BoxCast (Q73). Captions: the host's CC in More writes down what the phone hears, and every viewer reads the newest words on the picture, above Give, with CC there to hide them (Q74). Resi and BoxCast sell captions as a paid tier. Ours are free, but they ride the browser's speech engine on the host's phone, and nobody has tried that on a real phone that is also sending its camera and mic. A quiet service reads OFF AIR. Sunday morning sits on the picture, and a tap on the picture is Go live (Q59, Q46).

## Next gap

None of these are a 5. Go live now keeps every service, and Leave hands the host the replay to send (Q68, Q69). Resi and BoxCast also put that replay behind the same link. Someone who opens the link after it ended now reads that it has ended and plays the replay from any phone in the room that holds it (Q71). The host’s own kept copy is offered too, while the host’s replay stays open (Q72). Resi’s link plays it after everyone has gone. Ours does not: a replay that outlives every phone needs somewhere to live that is not a phone, and a 1-hour replay is a whole-file pull before it plays.
