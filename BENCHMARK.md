# Meeting and broadcast mobile scorecard

Scored from the mobile pass in `QUIRKS.md`, not from a feature checklist. Scale is 1–5. A 5 means that audience would rather use this than the product that usually wins for them. We do not lead any audience yet.

Competitors this is held against: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard, YouTube Live, and church streaming desks (Resi, BoxCast, ProPresenter-style “go live” panels).

## Where they still beat us

- **Zoom / Meet / Teams.** The hang-up, mute, and camera controls stay inside the screen on a 320px phone. Chat is a sheet or a column that does not sit under the call dock.
- **FaceTime.** One primary action, huge targets, no password essay, the preview is the screen.
- **StreamYard / YouTube Live.** The state is a banner now (Q14). They still win the program monitor and the screen a viewer gets when the broadcast has ended.
- **Church desks.** A volunteer names the service and goes on. Go live sits under the name, and Change host key sits under Watch (Q35). The key is still minted. A desk still does not list one.

The status line and the chip title grow with the root text size (Q6, Q13). Flip sits on the phone dock (Q5b). Create asks for one host password (Q12). The dock gap is at least 8px (Q10). Save is the only filled join action, and Clear blanks the name and the picture in the form (Q31).

Corporate, church, and personal are 4.0. None lead. A 5 would mean that audience would switch. Corporate is 4: the meeting lobby leads with Start a town hall, and the host key stays on that form (Q37). It is not a 5. People who join still land in a meeting. Warm copy for church is 4: a viewer opening a service is told they are watching and stay off camera (Q36). It is not a 5. Chat, the composer, and the lobby sentence already grow (Q21, Q24). FaceTime still wins that dimension. The rotated dock stops at 48px so the face stays a stage.
