# Meeting and broadcast mobile scorecard

Scored from the mobile pass in `QUIRKS.md`, not from a feature checklist. Scale is 1–5. A 5 means that audience would rather use this than the product that usually wins for them. We do not lead any audience yet.

Competitors this is held against: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard, YouTube Live, and church streaming desks (Resi, BoxCast, ProPresenter-style “go live” panels).

## Where they still beat us

- **Zoom / Meet / Teams.** The hang-up, mute, and camera controls stay inside the screen on a 320px phone. Chat is a sheet or a column that does not sit under the call dock. Reconnecting is a banner, not a truncated sentence.
- **FaceTime.** One primary action, huge targets, no password essay, the preview is the screen.
- **StreamYard / YouTube Live.** A broadcast is either LIVE or it is not. The host sees that before they look at a status sentence. Viewers joining late see “live” or “offline”, not “Joining…”.
- **Church desks.** A volunteer gets a short path: name the service, go on, read a link. The create action is on screen. Type stays large. Flip camera is a single tap during the service.

Hang up stays on screen (Q1). The chat field and Send sit above the dock, including when a keyboard covers the bottom half of an iPhone SE (Q2). Corporate and personal are 3.1. Church is 2.4. None lead. YouTube Live and StreamYard still win the church score because a broadcast never says LIVE or OFF AIR (Q5), and the create action is still below the fold (Q4).
