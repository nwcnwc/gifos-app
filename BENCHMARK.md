# Meeting and broadcast mobile scorecard

Scored from the mobile pass in `QUIRKS.md`, not from a feature checklist. Scale is 1–5. A 5 means that audience would rather use this than the product that usually wins for them. We do not lead any audience yet.

Competitors this is held against: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard, YouTube Live, and church streaming desks (Resi, BoxCast, ProPresenter-style “go live” panels).

## Where they still beat us

- **Zoom / Meet / Teams.** The hang-up, mute, and camera controls stay inside the screen on a 320px phone. Chat is a sheet or a column that does not sit under the call dock. Reconnecting is a banner, not a truncated sentence.
- **FaceTime.** One primary action, huge targets, no password essay, the preview is the screen.
- **StreamYard / YouTube Live.** A broadcast is either LIVE or it is not. The host sees that before they look at a status sentence. Viewers joining late see “live” or “offline”, not “Joining…”.
- **Church desks.** A volunteer gets a short path: name the service, go on, read a link. The words on that path stay short.

Hang up stays on screen (Q1). Chat sits above the dock, including under a keyboard (Q2). A broadcast now says RECONNECTING, OFF AIR, LIVE, or WATCHING (Q5). Create & enter stays in the card while the room fields scroll (Q4). More scrolls inside the screen in landscape (Q3). The chip and the dock clear a notch and a home indicator (Q7). The status line grows with the root text size (Q6). Flip sits on the phone dock (Q5b). Corporate and personal are 3.7. Church is 3.4. None lead. Landscape stays at 4: Meet is still the meeting a corporate user would rotate into, and a 5 would mean they would switch. The title in the chip is still a fixed pixel size, so large type is not a 5. FaceTime still wins the first screen on copy: Create still asks for two passwords. A first-visit name is still `Guest` plus digits (Q9) and the 320px dock gap is 4px (Q10). Those stay on the list behind the copy gap.
