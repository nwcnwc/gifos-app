# Meeting and broadcast mobile scorecard

Scored from the mobile pass in `QUIRKS.md`, not from a feature checklist. Scale is 1–5. A 5 means that audience would rather use this than the product that usually wins for them. We do not lead any audience yet.

Competitors this is held against: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard, YouTube Live, and church streaming desks (Resi, BoxCast, ProPresenter-style “go live” panels).

## Where they still beat us

- **Zoom / Meet / Teams.** The hang-up, mute, and camera controls stay inside the screen on a 320px phone. Chat is a sheet or a column that does not sit under the call dock.
- **FaceTime.** One primary action, huge targets, the preview is the screen. On a tall phone the picture is that screen, and a tap on it is Go live (Q59). Watch and the host key open from More. FaceTime still wins the call: one control, and almost nothing else on the glass.
- **StreamYard / YouTube Live.** The state is a banner now (Q14). The picture leads a phone before Go live (Q44). A quiet viewer reads that the service or stream has not started, and an emptied room reads that it has ended (Q54). A recording on the phone plays back (Q60). A speaker fills the phone, and the frame stays whole (Q61). The host can Share the video to whoever missed it (Q68). Go live keeps every service without a Record tap, and Leave hands the host that replay (Q69). The host sets a start time in More. The link says “starts Sunday at 10:00 AM” with Add to calendar, and an old link reads that it has ended (Q70). Someone who opens the link after it ended reads that, and nothing more. The Watch pull from phones in the room (Q71, Q72) is removed for upload reasons: anyone with the link could pull the whole file from one phone. The host's rebroadcast replaces it: Rebroadcast plays the last service, or a video file, into the broadcast through the live stage fan-out, and viewers read Prerecorded, never LIVE, with nothing to seek or save (Q76, Q77). A viewer reads Give on the picture, and Tip on a stream. It opens the host's own giving page, and it is still there on the screen that says the service ended (Q73).
- **Church desks.** A viewer’s join says they are seen only if they raise a hand and are brought on stage (Q52). Give sits on the picture and opens the church’s own giving page, as on Resi and BoxCast (Q73). Captions: the host's CC in More writes down what the phone hears, and every viewer reads the newest words on the picture, above Give, with CC there to hide them (Q74). Resi and BoxCast sell captions as a paid tier. Ours are free, but they ride the browser's speech engine on the host's phone, and nobody has tried that on a real phone that is also sending its camera and mic. A quiet service reads OFF AIR. Sunday morning sits on the picture, and a tap on the picture is Go live (Q59, Q46).

The chip and the dock clear a notch and a home indicator (Q7). The status line and the chip title grow with the root text size (Q6, Q13). Flip sits on the phone dock (Q5b). The host key is part of the room. It is minted and stored. These scores leave it out.

## Next gap

None of these are a 5. Go live now keeps every service, and Leave hands the host the replay to send (Q68, Q69). Resi and BoxCast also put that replay behind the same link. Someone who opens the link after it ended reads that it has ended. The replay behind the link (Q71, Q72) is removed: a whole-file pull from one phone grows that phone's upload with everyone who missed it (Q76). The host's rebroadcast, played through the live fan-out, replaces it. Resi's link plays the replay on demand. Ours plays what the host rebroadcasts, the last service or any file, as Prerecorded through the live fan-out, so the host's upload stays flat as viewers join (Q77). That matches Resi's simulated live and does not beat it: nothing plays unless the host's phone is in the room playing it.
