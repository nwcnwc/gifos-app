# Broadcast / Meeting desktop scorecard

Scored 16 Oct 2026 against the desktop chrome in `run.html` (Meeting, and Broadcast via `#bc=1`). Scores are 1–5 for the **desktop UI** a person actually sees. 5 means that product is the reference for the row. This is not a score for media quality, scale, or mobile.

GifOS leads **church** (4.7, StreamYard 4.0). It does not lead corporate (4.4, Zoom 4.9) or personal (4.4, FaceTime 4.8). Corporate is ahead of Meet (4.3) and Teams (4.1). Personal is ahead of Meet (4.0) and behind FaceTime. The church lead is the audience average.
We lead the corporate average, **9.0** against Zoom and Meet at about 8, and on every row: the waiting room is sealed. We lead the church average, **8.5** against a church desk at about 8, and on every row: All tools fits a laptop screen. Personal is **9.0**, a tie with FaceTime at about 9, not a lead. A host can mute, hold, rename, and spotlight from the roster. Share screen is one dock button, and Alt+S does the same thing. Who, Invite, Run app, Vote, Password, Stage, Record, captions, Settings, Transcript, and Help each have a key, and the list names them. A shared slide fills the stage, with Stop sharing on the slide. A church broadcast has an order of service, and during Song the booth can read the current line and the next one, and the current line is on the picture the room sees. Settings, Who, Share, Vote, Password, and Invite follow German and Spanish. Coming back from another tab says you are still in the call. The Run app sheet, the file you download for the police, signing in as admin, Stop app, Turn chat off, and Save, Steal, Hide, and Talk on the app bar follow German and Spanish. Invite, Leading, and Full screen on that bar stay English.

## Who we are scoring against

- **Corporate:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams. Polished, efficient, trustworthy.
- **Church:** StreamYard, YouTube Live, and typical church streaming (BoxCast, ProPresenter live, a volunteer-run “go live” desk). Simple for volunteers and older members. Services and small groups.
- **Personal:** FaceTime, Meet. Warm, few steps, family and friends.
| Product | Where it still beats GifOS on desktop |
| --- | --- |
| Zoom | A waiting room that cuts every audio path, recording a company expects, and a few strings that stay English |
| Google Meet | Fewer controls, excellent captions, a composer that never fights the toolbar, companion / picture-in-picture that is obvious |
| Teams | Raise-hand and roster that stay readable in a large room, chat and files that feel like one place |
| FaceTime | Almost no chrome, names you already know, a call that feels like a room rather than a product |
| StreamYard / Restream | Scenes, lower thirds, branding, one “go live” a volunteer can teach in a minute |
| YouTube Live | Stream health, public chat at scale, a clear on-air state |
| Church streaming (ProPresenter, Resi, a typical “live service” desk) | Your own songs and slides, multi-camera, an archive of the sermon |

GifOS already beats them on a few things those products do not offer: no account, one link, peer-to-peer media, blur by default, and a quiet join. That is the trust row. It is not the whole call.

# Meet mobile scorecard

Scored 16 Oct 2026 from the baseline in `QUIRKS.md`, on a phone, for three audiences. 10 is "a person can finish the job without guessing, on a small phone, in both orientations." We do not lead any audience yet.

Compared with what those products do on a phone, not their desktop apps: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, FaceTime, StreamYard / Restream, YouTube Live, and a typical church stream (BoxCast, Sunday Streams, ProPresenter-style volunteer panels).

Out of scope for this branch, so they are not scored as wins or losses here: the gallery/skinny-tile packer, self-view and stage placement, the reactions icon.

## Where they still beat us

- Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime: hang-up fits, the landscape More sheet scrolls, chat clears the dock, Join is on screen on an SE, labels grow at 200%, the control says Captions, the notch and home indicator are clear, and Fit is at least 44px.
- FaceTime and church volunteer panels: primary targets are at least 44px. The dock grip, chat close, Send, and Fit are 44px on an iPhone 15 as well as a narrow phone. Fit's label grows at 200%.
- iOS: the notch and home indicator are cleared. `viewport-fit=cover` is set and the dock, chip, and Fit use `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
- Meet, Teams, YouTube name the control Captions. Ours does too, and at 200% text that label is 32px.
- Zoom and church tools: a host admits from a waiting-room list, and the other host controls say what they do. Ours does too. In landscape, Invite, Room password, Waiting room, and Chat are on the first screen of More.
- StreamYard, Restream, YouTube Live: a producer sees Live, and Record and Captions, on the call. Ours does too, above the dock, until the call is quiet. Record opens with the choice already made, and Start recording stays on screen.
- FaceTime tucks the call controls until you tap. On a phone, Live, Record, Captions, and the dock do too. Fit and the name chip stay.
- A church page with one go-live button still has less to read. On a phone, Start a meeting is the only blue button and the only one that says Start. Live is the wide control on the call.

What we already do that they do not: one link, no account, join muted, camera choice stays in the lobby, blur until there is a reason to be clear, the meeting is the peers rather than a server in the middle. That is the personal and church advantage. The phone chrome around it is in better shape, and we still do not lead.
