Share your screen

Only this room’s hosts — or people they allow — can share a screen. Ask a host to allow you, on your tile or for the whole room, then open this again.

A screen share is for what an app can’t be: a deck on your disk, a tool that isn’t GifOS, a window from somewhere else. It shows on the stage, so the whole room sees it — and everyone can see that it’s you sharing.

Your camera stays on, and everyone sees your screen on the Stage. Sound from the shared tab doesn’t travel — talk over it.

    People

    There is no server in the middle storing this meeting. The phones and computers of the people in it are the servers: they connect straight to each other. That is also why everyone here can see everyone's internet address — nobody is anonymous. ✓ marks an address GifOS verified or this device saw directly; anything else is only what that person's device reported. People who left stay on the list. To report illegal activity to law enforcement, download this list and include it.

    How this meeting works

    Captions are on in this meeting. What you say may be written down by other people's devices.

    A public majority-rules meeting (open meeting) has no size limit; a private meeting holds as many people as its host's plan allows. The dream: the whole Earth in one meeting at once — everyone able to speak. Your phone talks only to a handful of neighbors.

    The room

    • Stage — step up to be seen and heard first by the whole room. Tap again to step down.
    • Stepping away? Tap Float before you pocket your phone or switch apps. The meeting keeps running in a small window and you keep your place on the Stage. Without it, your phone may pause the page and take you off.
    • Row — the people next to you.
    • The stadium — everyone else, one mosaic and one roar.

    Controls

    • Camera — tap it to choose Max blur, Min blur, or No blur. Only you control your camera.
    • Hand — join the queue.
    • Password — the key to clear video.
    • Invite — one link brings people in.
    • Run app — an app the room uses together.
    • Record / CC — record here, or captions from your voice.
    • Settings — mix for Stage, My row, and the stadium. Timing: Conversation, Unison, or Song.
    • Host — in a private meeting, the host can moderate.

    Singing together

    Tap Song to lead. You step onto the stage as the song leader, and the room lines up so you are singing together, like a large hall.
