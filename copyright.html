GifOS copyright

GifOS respects copyright. If something shown through GifOS (a room, an app, or a listing on gifos.tv) uses your work without permission, you can tell us and we will act on a complete notice.

Send a notice

The quickest way is inside GifOS: open Report (a meeting's More menu, an embed's info panel, or a gifos.tv listing's ⋯ menu) and choose Copyright or trademark. Or email support@gifos.app. Either way, include:

  • the work you say was copied, and that you own it or may act for the owner;
  • where it appears (the room, app or listing link);
  • your name (typed as your signature), and an email address where we can reach you;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  • a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

We may pass a notice, with your contact details, to the person it is about. Do not send one unless you mean it: knowingly false notices can make you liable for damages.

Counter-notice

If we removed or disabled something of yours because of a notice and you believe that was a mistake, email support@gifos.app with a counter-notice: what was removed and where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification, your name, address and phone number, your consent to the courts of your district (or of California if you are outside the US), and your signature. We will pass it to the person who sent the notice, and may restore the item after ten to fourteen business days unless they tell us they have gone to court.

Repeat infringers

We end the access of people who are repeatedly the subject of valid copyright notices.
