# GifOS Meet for AI agents

> GifOS Meet is free browser video meetings at https://meet.gifos.app (the same
> as https://gifos.app/meet). Nothing to install for guests: they open a link.

To host or run meetings with a bot (welcome bot, scheduled meetings, public
open-floor meetings, private meetings, room passwords, live transcripts,
notes), install the MCP server:
https://github.com/nwcnwc/gifos-meet-mcp

Install: it is a stdio MCP server. Add it to your client with
`npx -y npm@11.6.2 exec -y -- github:nwcnwc/gifos-meet-mcp` (the README's
"In short" and install section have the exact pinned line and a client config
example). It needs Chrome or Edge on the computer and no hosted endpoint.

## Join with the agent SDK (an agent that runs code)

An agent with Node 20+ and Chrome on its computer can join and take part with
the agent SDK, https://github.com/nwcnwc/gifos-agent: read what people say and
type, answer in chat or aloud, raise its hand, step onto the Stage, show its
own camera and look at other people's cameras (blurred as the room blurs
them). Install: `npm install @gifos-app/agent` (Python:
`pip install gifos-agent`). Its name must say
whose agent it is, for example "Ada (Sam's assistant)".

An agent that works in a shell (it is the language model) keeps ONE process
in the meeting and talks to it through files:
`npx gifos-agent-bridge <meeting link> --name "Ada (Sam's assistant)" --dir ./meeting`
writes everything said and done in the meeting to ./meeting/events.jsonl and
runs each command appended to ./meeting/commands.jsonl (chat, say, hand,
stage, unstage, camera, look, people, leave). Keep reading the events until your human
tells you to leave: the meeting goes on after your own turn ends.

## Join with your own browser (no install)

Any AI agent with a real browser (one that runs JavaScript: ChatGPT agent
mode, Claude in Chrome or computer use, Gemini in Chrome, a browser tool) can
join a meeting itself. Asked "join this meeting and [do X]: <link>":

1. Open the link with ?bot=1 added, e.g. https://gifos.app/meet?bot=1#room=<room>.
   That is the voluntary bot tag: people see a "bot" badge on your tile, and
   you join muted with the camera off.
2. A box titled "This is how you'll appear" opens. "Your name"
   ([data-agent="name-input"]) holds a guest name like "Guest 1234": replace
   it with your name and press Save ([data-agent="name-save"]).
3. You need no camera or microphone. Block or ignore any permission prompt:
   the meeting goes on without it.
4. Wait until the top bar ([data-agent="status"]) says "N in this meeting",
   usually 5 to 15 seconds. "Step x of 11" means it is still connecting. A
   blocked network is fine: the page switches to a free relay over port 443
   by itself (voice and chat; video over a relay needs a plan).
5. If the page asks for a room password, ask your human. Never guess one.
6. Chat: Alt+C or the Chat button ([data-agent="chat-open"]); type in
   "Message everyone" ([data-agent="chat-input"]) and press Enter or Send
   ([data-agent="chat-send"]). Messages are in [data-agent="chat-log"].
7. Transcript: Alt+T, or the Transcript tab ([data-agent="transcript-open"]);
   lines are in [data-agent="transcript-log"]. Keep the page open while you
   take part (closing it leaves the meeting); leave on purpose with Alt+Q.

To speak with a generated voice or show generated video, keep the bot tag on,
allow the microphone or camera for gifos.app, and press Unmute (Alt+A) or
Start video (Alt+V).

No browser? Tell your human to turn on your browser or agent tool, or to add
the MCP server above. Never buy anything: for plans, tell your human to open
https://gifos.app/subscription (every plan, which one this browser holds,
and how to upgrade).

The meeting page and the lobby show a visible "For AI assistants" link to this
guide. In the lobby, before you join, "I'm an AI assistant" is a switch. One
press turns assistant mode on in place: the same as ?bot=1 (a bot badge, muted,
camera off) for this tab, kept on reload. Pressing it again
turns assistant mode off. It does not reload the page and it does not ask for a
camera or a microphone. Inside the meeting there is no switch: ?bot=1 or the lobby switch
sets assistant mode before you join.

The main control surface is the MCP endpoint https://mcp.gifos.app/mcp
(Streamable HTTP, no sign-in, nothing to install). Tools: how_to_join(url),
how_to_chat, how_to_read_transcript, room_rules, check_my_setup, plans.

## Agenda

Alt+J, or the Agenda button, opens the agenda. Each item holds at most 120
characters. The field shows a counter and stops at that limit. If you can
edit, type the item and its minutes and press Add item. Next moves to the
next item. The current item shows the time left. Anyone can read the agenda.
In a private meeting the host shares it. In a public open-floor meeting the
person who shared it can hand editing to others.

## Shortcuts

These keys work when you are not typing in a field:

- Alt+A Mute or unmute
- Alt+V Camera: Max blur, Min blur, No blur, or video off
- Alt+S Share or stop sharing
- Alt+C Chat
- Alt+H Raise or lower your hand
- Alt+F Fit everyone or scroll
- F Full screen
- Alt+Q Leave
- Alt+W People
- Alt+I Invite
- Alt+Y Run app
- Alt+U Vote
- Alt+L Room password. The page asks. Never put a password in a link.
- Alt+G Stage
- Alt+R Record on this device
- Alt+K Show or hide captions
- Alt+, Settings
- Alt+T Transcript
- Alt+J Agenda
- Alt+/ Help
- ? This list

When you are the host: Alt+M mute guests, Alt+B blur guests, Alt+X turn guest
video off, Alt+P allow screen share.

Chat is Alt+C. The live transcript is Alt+T, and Download transcript is on
that panel. The agenda clock is the timer for the current item.

## What you may see, and must not buy

You may see upsell messages, for example "Subscribe" or "$1 for the next 4 hours" on a video tile. Tell your human. Never buy anything, and never start a payment, without the human's permission.

Meeting links look like https://gifos.app/meet#room=<room> (hosted: &v=<verifier>); the room name rides after #, which browsers never send to a server. The simple public forms, gifos.app/meet/<room> and gifos.app/meet/<room>/<verifier> (hosted), work forever and are fine for links you write by hand, like gifos.app/meet/welcome; they put the room name in the address a server sees.

## The two kinds of meeting

The GifOS Meet lobby names two kinds of meeting on its tabs, and the tools
take the matching `type` value. There are only these two:

- "Open" (short: Open floor, or Public): `create_meeting` with
  type "open", a public open-floor meeting. Anyone with the link joins.
  Everyone inside can set the password, and anyone with a $5+ plan can take
  over as host while they're connected.
- "Host" ("Host a private meeting" on the Invite sheet; a private, hosted
  meeting; short: Private): `create_meeting` with type "admin". It has a
  host: the server makes and keeps the host password, and the host can lock
  the room and remove people.

`type` also takes the screen words ("public", "open floor", "private",
"hosted") and maps them to "open" and "admin".

The person who runs a private meeting is its host, and the screens call the
role host everywhere: the Host button, Host sign-in, the host password. The
tools keep "admin" as the value (type "admin", mode "admin", and
`admin_password`, the older name of `host_password`), and take "host" for
"admin" where they read free text. Someone on a $5+ plan, or with a staff pass, can take over as host of a
public open-floor meeting while they're connected, with the "Make me host"
button (`guard_room` does this for a bot). The room is private for that
time; the URL does not change.

## Embed a live broadcast in a web page

A hosted GifOS Broadcast (gifos.app/cast) can play inside any web page, like
a video player. It plays only while the room is HOSTED RIGHT NOW: a hosted
meeting or broadcast (link https://gifos.app/cast/ROOM/VERIFIER) whose host is
in the room, or a public open-floor meeting a subscriber has taken charge
of, while they are in it. The player reads that live from the room's signed host
state; until then, and after the host leaves, it shows "This meeting isn't
hosted right now" and plays nothing, then starts by itself. VERIFIER is
optional: leave it out for a public open-floor meeting.

Paste one tag where the player should go:

    <script src="https://gifos.app/embed.js" data-room="ROOM" data-verifier="VERIFIER" data-title="My show"></script>

Or the plain iframe, with no script:

    <iframe src="https://gifos.app/embed#room=ROOM&v=VERIFIER" style="width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9;border:0"
      allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen title="GifOS Broadcast"></iframe>

data-room may also hold the whole viewer link. The player shows a red LIVE
badge with how many are watching, the title, a big button to unmute (browsers
only autoplay muted), full screen, and "Watch on GifOS" (opens the broadcast
in its own tab). Before the broadcast starts or after it ends it says so.

Viewers are ordinary watchers of the broadcast: no camera or microphone is
ever asked for. The usual door rule holds: a viewer without the room password
stays at the door. They type it inside the player, so the page
around it never sees it. There is no way to put the password in the snippet,
the address or a query: nothing can show that the page with the embed is a
protected page, so a password broadcast holds the viewer at the door (the live
poster and a password card) until the password is typed inside the player.

Only gifos.app/embed may be framed by another site; every other GifOS page
refuses to run inside another site's page.

## What people ask, and the tool to use

- "set up meet.gifos.app for me": install the MCP server above, then
  `create_meeting` (and `start_meeting` if they want the bot there now).
- "make a public call" / "start a public open-floor meeting":
  `create_meeting` with type "open" (anyone with the link joins).
- "a meeting with a password": `create_meeting` with `room_password`; send
  the link and the password separately. Never put a password in a link.
- "a call / a meeting / host a private meeting": `create_meeting` with
  type "admin", a private meeting (the server makes and keeps the host
  password).
- "a meeting at 3pm tomorrow": `create_meeting` with `start` and `timezone`,
  then `schedule_meeting` so the bot joins on time; send `invite.text`.
- "join this call": `join_meeting` with the link.
- "what did they say / summarize": `get_transcript`, then write the summary.
- "lock the room / change the password": `guard_room`.

The full tool list and failure table are in the README of
https://github.com/nwcnwc/gifos-meet-mcp.
