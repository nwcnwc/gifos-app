GifOS

Make your own app for meetings

A meeting is more than faces in boxes. Bring an app and everyone in the meeting uses it together: the same board, the same list, the same turn, live, with voice and video around it. In GifOS an app is one GIF file, so the app you make is something you can send, keep and remix. No server, no account, no app store needed.

You don't need to know how to code. Tell your AI what you want, and it builds the GIF for you.

The guide your AI reads →

1. Ask your AI to build it

Give Claude (or any AI) this link: gifos.app/llms.txt. It is the complete recipe: how to pack an app into a GIF, the manifest, and every window.gifos call. Then say what you want, for example:

  • "A GifOS meeting app where everyone adds sticky notes and votes on them."
  • "A quiz for our team meeting: I ask, everyone answers, scores add up."
  • "A shared checklist for our stand-up, ticked live by everyone."

If the AI can run code, it hands you a finished .gif file. Give it your favourite GIF and it hides the app inside that exact animation. If it can only write code, paste the code into ＋ Add on your Home Screen and GifOS packs it for you.

2. Make it shared

An app is multiplayer by default. Your one job is to say which data everyone shares. In the app's manifest.json:

"capabilities": { "db": true, "multiplayer": true },
"data": {
  "notes": { "visibility": "read-write" },
  "prefs": { "visibility": "private" }
}

read-write is shared by everyone in the meeting, private stays with each person. Draw your screen from db.subscribe(), and GifOS does the rest: syncing, late joiners, reconnects. Want the host to steer, like a presenter turning pages? Add "lead". All of it is in llms.txt under "Multiplayer".

3. Run it in your meeting

In a meeting, press Run app, then Choose a GIF file, or paste a link to your GIF. It opens on the stage for everyone. This works on gifos.app in any modern browser and in the GifOS Meet app on Android.

Look at what already ships for ideas: Take Turns (who talks next, with a gentle timer) and Timer (a stopwatch and countdown).

4. Share it

Your app is a file. Post the GIF anywhere a picture goes, send it in a chat, or put it online and share the link: anyone can paste that link into Run app. Sign it so people know it's yours, remix other people's apps, and, when it's ready, ask for a place in the App Store. The "Sharing", "Signing" and "Modding" parts of llms.txt tell you how.

Good to know

  • Apps run in a sandbox with no network access unless they declare the sites they need, and every person sees what an app asks for.
  • An app never touches anyone's camera or microphone. The meeting does the audio and video; your app is the shared thing in the middle.
  • In the GifOS Meet phone apps, an app in a meeting can't take payments: there, only GifOS's own plans are sold, through the phone's store.
