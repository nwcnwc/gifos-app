GifOS privacy

Effective: 13 November 2026.

Every GifOS app is built so that we hold as little about you as possible. There are no accounts: no sign-up, email address, phone number, contacts or advertising identifier, and no ads or third-party tracking. Meetings and other live sharing are end-to-end encrypted between the people in them. The meeting server (relay) and the fallback relay (TURN) see the IP addresses of the connections they pass, which every internet connection reveals, and write no log of clients. Where a plan or purchase is offered, our payment service keeps only a purchase identifier, the plan and its dates, and a public key made on your device, never your name, email or card details. Each app's own policy lists the details that apply to it.

Policies by app

  • GifOS Meet: meetings on the web (gifos.app/meet), the Android app and the iPhone app.
  • GifOS Broadcast: its policy will be added here when that app ships.
