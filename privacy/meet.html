GifOS Meet privacy policy

Effective: 13 November 2026.

GifOS Meet is built so that we hold as little about you as possible. There are no accounts. This policy covers GifOS Meet on the web (gifos.app/meet), the GifOS Meet app for Android and the GifOS Meet app for iPhone, and the plans offered for them. Other GifOS apps have their own policies; see the GifOS privacy index.

What we never ask for

No sign-up, no email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, and no advertising identifier. GifOS Meet has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Meetings

  • Audio, video, chat and files go between the people in the meeting. WebRTC encrypts them in transit. Our servers do not record or store them. The meeting server never carries audio or video.
  • The meeting server (relay) introduces people in a room to each other. It passes along the connection messages between them and sees temporary room and participant identifiers derived from the meeting link, and your IP address, which every internet connection reveals. It keeps nothing: a room exists only while people are in it, and the relay writes no request log and stores no address. The meeting's Who panel shows each participant's network address to the other participants, because a direct connection reveals it to the person on the other end.
  • The fallback relay (TURN) is used only when a direct connection is impossible. It passes encrypted media between two participants without being able to decrypt it, and sees the IP addresses of both ends while it does so. It writes no log of clients and keeps no address on disk. Its only record is a count of tickets issued and refused, which holds no address and no room name.
  • Connectivity checks (STUN). To find a direct path, your device asks public STUN servers for its public IP address. Those requests carry no meeting content.
  • Your display name is stored on your device and sent only to the other people in the meeting.
  • Camera and microphone. In the apps, Android and iOS ask for permission the first time you turn them on; on the web, your browser does. You join every meeting muted with the camera off. The camera turns on only when you switch it on.
  • Captions. While your microphone is on, the meeting writes what you say down as captions, and only the caption text goes to the meeting (Settings → Captions turns sending off). A speech engine on your device makes the text, chosen in Settings → Captions. Browser captions, the default and the only engine in the Android app, use your browser's or phone's own speech service, which may send your voice to its maker to turn it into text (Chrome and Android phones: Google; Safari and iPhone: Apple) under that company's privacy policy. GifOS never receives the audio. Whisper runs on your computer and sends your voice nowhere; its speech model is downloaded once from gifos.app. It is not offered on phones.
  • Recordings are saved on your device. GifOS Meet does not upload them anywhere.
  • Debug logs stay on your device unless you export and send one yourself.

Plans and payments

GifOS Meet offers the Room 20 and Unlimited plans, the $1 pass and tips. They are handled by our payment service (pay.gifos.app). On the web, PayPal handles the payment and its privacy policy applies. In the Android app and the iPhone app, where a purchase is offered, Google Play or the App Store handles it under Google's or Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive your card details, name or email address.

For each plan the payment service keeps one record: the subscription or purchase identifier (for a purchase through Google Play or the App Store, the store's purchase token or transaction identifier), the plan and its period, the status and paid-through date, your public subscription key (made on your device, not your name), and the tag of the one meeting your plan was last used in. A free plan has the same record without any payment identifier. To limit abuse, the service counts how many free plans are made from one network address in a day; it keeps only the times of those requests under a name derived from the address.

The service also writes one log line for each payment event (started, captured, refused, refunded), carrying the payment provider's reference. Cloudflare keeps these log lines for a few days (3 on its free plan, 7 on its paid plan). The payment providers keep their own records under their own policies.

If you email us (for example to ask for a refund, as the terms allow within 14 days of a purchase made directly from us), we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you and handle your request.

Who hosts this

gifos.app and its services run on Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare handles the network traffic to reach us, under its own privacy policy.

Data on your device

Settings, your display name and meeting preferences are stored in the app's (or browser's) own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes them.

Children

GifOS Meet is for adults. It is not for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date. Questions: support@gifos.app.
