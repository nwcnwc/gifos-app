      Changing to a bigger plan

      A bigger plan replaces yours. Its first charge is smaller: we take off a credit for the unused time of your current plan. Every charge after that is the full price.

      We cancel the plan it replaces as soon as the new one starts, so you are never billed twice.

      A yearly plan can change only to a bigger yearly plan. A monthly plan can change to a bigger monthly or yearly plan.

      Plans add up: a pass or an add-on you hold counts on top of your plan, so their meetings at once add together.

      A four-hour pass is never cancelled: it keeps running beside any plan.
