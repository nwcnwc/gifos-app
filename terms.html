GifOS terms of service

Effective: 13 November 2026.

GifOS is made by Crowd Concepts, Inc., California, USA. Each GifOS app has its own terms of service, written for what that app really does. They share the same ground: there are no accounts, meetings and live sharing are end-to-end encrypted so we cannot see their content, paid plans are billed by PayPal on the web or by Google Play or the App Store in the apps, and you keep your own credentials on your device. How we handle data is in the GifOS privacy policies.
