Meetings

  • Public majority-rules meetings (open meetings; a link with no host part) are free and have no people limit. Anyone with the link can join.
  • Private, hosted meetings are run with a host password. The host can lock the meeting and remove people. On the free plan a private, hosted meeting holds up to 5 people; the Room 20 plan raises that to 20, and the Unlimited plan removes the limit.
  • GifOS's own rooms. No plan or pass lets a person take charge of a public majority-rules meeting. GifOS's own bots may hold a GifOS room (such as the welcome room) as its host: set its password and remove people. If you need control of a meeting, start a private, hosted meeting.
