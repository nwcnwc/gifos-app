Rooms

  • Open rooms (a link with no admin part) are free and have no people limit. Anyone with the link can join.
  • Admin rooms are hosted with an admin password. The admin can lock the room and remove people. On the free plan an admin room holds up to 5 people; the Room 20 plan raises that to 20, and the Unlimited plan removes the limit.
  • Open-room capture. A person on the Unlimited plan (or on a Streamer plan from the Broadcast app) who is in an open room can take it over as its admin: set a password and remove people. The capture ends when that person's plan stops being used in that meeting (for example, they leave). If you meet in an open room, it can be captured this way. If you need control of a room, host an admin room.

Plans, passes and tips

GifOS Meet itself is free: open rooms, admin rooms of up to 5 people, and audio for everyone even on networks that block direct connections. The paid options add more:

  • Room 20, $2 a month or $20 a year: admin rooms of up to 20 people, and video through our relay servers on networks that block direct connections.
  • Unlimited, $5 a month or $50 a year: admin rooms with no people limit, video through our relay servers, and open-room capture.
  • The four-hour pass, $1 once: the Unlimited plan's meeting rights (not open-room capture) for 4 hours from purchase. Passes stack: a pass bought while you still hold pass time starts when that time ends.
  • Tips to GifOS, any amount offered. A tip is a thank-you; it buys nothing and grants no feature.

Prices are in US dollars; in the apps the store shows the price in your currency, with any tax it adds. A plan or pass covers one meeting at a time: using it in a new meeting moves it there, and the meeting it left returns to the free plan's limits within a few minutes. Video through our relay servers is shared capacity: at busy times it may be slowed, and video slows down before audio.
