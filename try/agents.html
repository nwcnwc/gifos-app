How it works

  1. Start a meeting at gifos.app/meet and copy its link.
  2. Hand your agent the link. A shell agent can keep one process in the meeting and talk to it through files:
    npx gifos-agent-bridge <link> \
  --name "Ada (Sam's assistant)" --dir ./meeting
  3. Talk to it. Everything said and typed lands in events.jsonl; it answers by appending to commands.jsonl (chat, say, hand, stage, look, leave).
