Why it works for you

  • One file is the appThe GIF carries the whole app. It runs sandboxed with no server to run, and it is multiplayer by default, so a meeting can use it together.
  • Paid direct, 97/3Each sale is two USDC transfers on Base: 97% to the address in your signed manifest, 3% to GifOS. GifOS never holds your money, and you are the seller of record.
  • Reach people where they meetRun app puts your GIF in front of a whole meeting at once. Ask for a place in the App Store; any signed app can take payments, listed or not.

How it works

  1. Build it, or have your AI build it from gifos.app/llms.txt: the packing recipe, the manifest and the window.gifos API.
  2. Price it in the manifest, add your Base payout address under "pay", and charge with gifos.charge. Buyers approve each charge on their own device.
  3. Sign it at gifos.app/sign and share the GIF anywhere, or ask for a place in the App Store.
Publishing is free; GifOS keeps 3% of each sale. In-app payments are real money: buyers pay in USDC on the Base network from their own wallet. Purchases become receipt files on the buyer's device, with no accounts anywhere. See every plan.
