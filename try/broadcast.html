Why it works for you

  • Nothing for viewers to set upA viewer opens the link and watches. Watching never asks for a camera or a microphone.
  • Your site, your stagePaste one script tag and the player appears: the picture, a LIVE badge, unmute, and the controls you choose (volume, captions, full screen, picture in picture).
  • Bring people upViewers raise a hand and you bring them onto the Stage. Questions get votes, chat flows live, and the chat is gone when the broadcast ends.

How it works

  1. Name your broadcast and go live at gifos.app/cast.
  2. Share the viewer link, or paste the player on your site:
    <script src="https://gifos.app/embed.js"
  data-room="ROOM"></script>
    It plays while you are hosting, and shows a short card the rest of the time.
  3. Run the show. Video starts blurred for safety: viewers tap to see the Stage clearly, and a room password clears everything for the people you give it to.
