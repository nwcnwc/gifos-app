Why it works for you

  • Scripture open for everyoneRun the Bible app (139 public-domain Bibles, offline from the first verse) or Bible Study (Henry, Calvin and the church fathers beside the text) inside the meeting.

How it works

  1. Start a meeting and send the link to your group, by text or email.
  2. Press Run app and open the Bible. Everyone follows along on their own screen.
  3. Share and pray. Use the chat for prayer requests, raise a hand to speak, and download the transcript afterwards if you like.
