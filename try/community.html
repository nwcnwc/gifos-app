Why it works for you

  • Free at any sizeOpen meetings are always free and have no people limit. Share one link with everyone.
  • Majority rulesOne vote each, while you are in the room. A majority brings someone up, sends them down, or removes them.
  • Made for crowdsSpeakers on the Stage, everyone else in rows, a hand queue, questions with votes, and reactions. Anyone inside can lock the room with a password.

How it works

  1. Start an open meeting and post the link where your community gathers.
  2. People join muted, from any browser. Speakers come up to the Stage by vote or by raising a hand.
  3. The room keeps order. Anyone can mute or blur a disruptive person for the room, and a majority can vote them out.
Majority-rules meetings are always free, at any size. Want one person in charge for a while? Someone on Unlimited ($5 a month) can take charge of an open meeting while they are connected. See every plan.
