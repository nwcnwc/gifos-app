Why it works for you

  • Nothing to hand overNo email address, no phone number, no contacts, no advertising ID. A paid plan lives on your device, not in an account.
  • End-to-end encryptedVoice, video and chat go between the people in the meeting. Our servers introduce you and get out of the way.
  • Your camera, your callYou choose when your camera goes on and how blurred you look. Captions are made on your own device, and a recording stays on the device that made it.

How it works

  1. Start a meeting and send the link. Nobody needs anything else.
  2. Meet. Your device talks to the other devices, encrypted end to end.
  3. Leave. Chat lives only in the meeting. Nothing waits on a server for you to come back.

Good to know: as with any direct call, the people in a meeting can see each other's network addresses.
