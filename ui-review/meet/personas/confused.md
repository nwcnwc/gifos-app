# Confused first visit — Meeting

Aurora, light page. Wide screen and phone. The "reconnecting" words in the bar were this review's closed relay (`ws://127.0.0.1:9`), so that tail is not a product claim. The rest is what the screen actually showed.

The only button that looks tappable is the blue "Start a meeting". Under it: "Open room · anyone with the link can join". Next is "Start a room you run" ("You're the admin: set a password, mute/blur, ban a troublemaker"), then "Join a link". I would hit the blue one even if a friend had sent me a link. "Meeting" and "room" sound like the same thing, and "room you run" sounds like work.
High. Say "Start an open meeting" and "Start a private room", and make "Join a link" just as big.

"Start a room you run" asks for two passwords. "Admin password — yours alone — do not share it" and "Guest password — the one you do share". The guest note says faces stay fully blurred (Max) until I set one, and talks about a Blur slider and a Password button that are not on this page. I would put the same word in both boxes, or skip the guest box and not know why.
High. Ask for one guest password, in one sentence, and don't mention blur here.

I left "Room name (anything you like)" empty and tapped "Create & enter". It never said the name was missing. It only said "Set an admin password — it's the key to your room." "Create & enter" is a plain gray button beside "Cancel", so I almost hit "Cancel".
Med. Tell me the name is required, and make "Create & enter" the blue button.

"Join a link" looks optional, so I would skip it. The box says "Paste link or meeting id". "see you at 3", a Zoom link, and "not a link!!" each got "Couldn't read that — paste a meeting link or its id." That line is pale pink on white and I almost missed it. Then I typed hello. No error. It opened a meeting, and "hello" showed up under "RECENT & SAVED".
High. Treat a bare word like a bad paste, show a dark-red error, and give one example of a real id.

The door says "You'll join as Guest" with a tiny "change". I would skip it. In the meeting a card says "Pick a screen name", placeholder "Your name", buttons "Not now" and "Continue". I would tap "Not now" and stay "Guest" plus some numbers.
Med. Ask for my name once, on this door, and don't make "Not now" the easy tap.

Before I was really in, my picture was badged "muted", "max blur", and "camera off". The bar said "Just you — send the invite; the link has no expiry · reconnecting to the room…". I thought it had failed. On the phone that line is cut off at "reconnecting". The words I can see are "Max", "Min", "No blur", and "Leave". Nothing says Invite until I find a chevron.
High. Say "Still connecting" in the bar, show "Invite" without another tap, and don't lead with blur.

The joining sheet says "Reaching the meeting…" and "Connecting you to the room." On this light theme those words are dark gray on a near-black sheet, so the title almost disappears (the line under it is only a bit better). On the wide screen my big "G" sits on top of "Reaching the meeting…", so I cannot read it. The name card also covers those words until I tap "Not now". On the phone the sentence is under the picture and I can just make it out.
High. Put that message on a light card, in dark type, above the picture and above the name card.

A tiny "edge" sits in the corner. I don't know if it is a button.
Low. Hide it on this door.
