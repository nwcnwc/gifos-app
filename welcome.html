GifOS is a free web computer where every app is a GIF, with meetings and broadcasts that run between the people in them, not on our servers. Below is the welcome meeting, live, playing on this page the way it can play on yours.

Put a live GifOS broadcast on your own site

Paste one line where the player should go. Put your room's name in place of ROOM. For a private meeting, add its verifier (the last part of its link, gifos.app/cast/ROOM/VERIFIER) as data-verifier, or &v=VERIFIER in the iframe. The player plays while a host is in charge of the room and shows a short card the rest of the time. Viewers never turn on a camera or microphone.

The script tag (works on most sites)

<script src="https://gifos.app/embed.js" data-room="ROOM"></script>

The plain iframe (for sites that remove scripts)

<iframe src="https://gifos.app/embed#room=ROOM" style="width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9;border:0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen title="GifOS Broadcast"></iframe>

Choose the controls

The player starts as the picture, the live badge, unmute, and at the bottom right the buttons for chat, reactions, info, float and full screen beside a settings gear. The bar fades after a few seconds and comes back on any pointer, tap or key. When you set nothing, the player offers volume,audioonly,fullscreen,pip,chat,reactions.

volume — a volume slider in the gear.

mute — a mute switch in the gear.

captions — a captions switch in the gear. It appears once a line has arrived. Captions stay off until the viewer turns them on.

audioonly — an audio-only switch in the gear: the viewer's screen stops drawing the pictures and the sound goes on.

tabs — the share tabs, when the room shares something besides the Stage.

inset — show, hide or move the Stage picture while a share fills the player.

fullscreen — the full screen button.

pip — the float button (picture in picture), when the browser supports it.

chat — the chat button, while the host leaves chat on. A viewer gives a name the first time they post.

reactions — the reactions button, while the host lets guests react.

watch — Watch on GifOS, in a new tab.

all turns every control on. none leaves only the info button.

On the script tag, add data-controls="tabs,inset,captions,fullscreen,watch". On the iframe, add it to the hash: #room=ROOM&controls=tabs,inset. An iframe you write yourself keeps allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" and allowfullscreen, or the full screen and float buttons do not show.

The password prompt, the not-hosted notice and the info button (the full title and a line about GifOS) always show.
